How the score works

Each hour starts from daylight: deer move most in the hour around sunrise and sunset. Then it adds or subtracts for the rut phase (midday cruising during chasing and breeding), solunar periods (moon overhead, underfoot, rising or setting), a temperature drop from yesterday, pressure trend, wind, rain or snow, and a bright moon. Stands add how well the spot type fits the current phase and how the forecast wind lines up with where deer approach. It is a planning guide built on common hunting research and lore, not a guarantee.

Weather: Open-Meteo, refreshed every hour while the app is open. Moon and sun: calculated on your device.
